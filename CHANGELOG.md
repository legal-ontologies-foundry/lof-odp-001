# Changelog

All notable changes are recorded here. Versions follow semantic versioning
(LOF-P-006).

## [Unreleased] - proposed for 0.3.0

### Added

- Norm-generating practice (custom, precedent) as a source of legal content alongside legal acts; nullity: purported, void and voidable acts, annulment and declarations of nullity.
- Tested with the cross-regime test suite (14 legal regimes, 85 competency questions) on the released 0.2.0 patterns: all pass.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-14

- Added `performed by means of` (ODP001_0001103): a legal act to the legal document that is its instrument; a sub-relation of BFO has participant.
- Added `evidences` (ODP001_0001104): a material entity to the act or content it records or attests to; not a form of participation.
- Added three kinds of legal content: `legal norm` (ODP001_0001005), `legal definition` (ODP001_0001006), and `legal designator` (ODP001_0001007), with CCO correspondences. Issue #10 (W. Mandrick).
- Documented the instrument and evidence distinction, with new competency questions. Issues #6 and #8 (W. Mandrick).

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-02

- First draft for review by the LOF founding coordinators.
